Settings

Make one at zotero.org → Settings → Security → Create new private key, with “Allow library access” and “Allow write access”. It is kept in this browser only and sent only to api.zotero.org.

Every collection with this name is a queue – under a parent collection, the parent’s first word labels it (F, M, T …), and you can filter by it. Read and Keep move an item into the parent collection (or just out, at the top) and tag it “from: <collection>”; Reject puts it in the trash, tagged “rejected: <reason>”.

Your library’s proxy address (EZproxy and the like) with %u where the article’s address goes. Each item then has a link “Full text via library” – the publisher’s page through your library’s subscriptions (you log in there).

Sources

Searches and feeds whose new items are put in the collection when the app opens (every 6 hours) – nothing already in your library or its trash. They are kept in a Zotero note, so all your devices share them.

Most journals’ own feeds can’t be read from a web page – use the journal’s ISSN instead, or a proxy: an address the feed’s address is put after (your own, e.g. the small Cloudflare Worker in this app’s repository; it sees which feeds you read). PubMed feeds and the searches need none.
